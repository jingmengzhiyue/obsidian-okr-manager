# Vault OKR Manager 1.4.0 — Weighted execution health and structured reviews

Version 1.4.0 adds weighted key results, explainable execution health, and structured reviews. Prioritize the results that matter most, spot risks before the deadline, and preserve the context behind your decisions in your vault.

**Requires Obsidian 1.7.2 or later.** Existing 1.3.x Objective files work without manual migration. English and Simplified Chinese interfaces are supported.

## Highlights

### KR weights and weighted Objective progress

Every KR now has a positive relative weight. Objective progress uses those weights instead of treating every result as equally important. Weights are normalized automatically, so values such as `2, 1, 1` become 50%, 25%, and 25%; no manual total of 100 is required.

Existing Objective files remain compatible. A KR without a stored weight behaves as weight `1`, which preserves the previous equal-average result until you choose to rebalance it.

### Health that explains execution risk

Progress and health are now separate. Progress continues to report measurable completion, while health evaluates whether work is likely to remain on track by considering:

- expected progress along the created-to-due timeline;
- high, medium, or low confidence;
- the blocker reported by the latest check-in;
- on-hold status;
- overdue and incomplete work.

Dashboard cards, KR rows, and Objective detail tables show a health score and one of three operating states: **On track**, **At risk**, or **Off track**. Hovering a badge reveals the active reasons, making the score auditable instead of opaque.

### A structured review rhythm in Markdown

The new **Period reviews** command and period-menu entry provide three purpose-built workflows:

- **Weekly review** — repeatable by date, focused on progress, wins, blockers, and next steps.
- **Mid-cycle review** — one per period, focused on achievements, risks, adjustments, and decisions.
- **Cycle retrospective** — one per period, focused on outcomes, what worked, lessons, and follow-up actions.

Creating a review captures an immutable snapshot of Objective and KR progress, status, weight, normalized share, health score, expected progress, and risk reasons. Later edits update only the review narrative, preserving the historical evidence behind the discussion.

Review files are ordinary Markdown under `<OKR root>/<period>/Reviews/`. Custom text outside plugin-managed blocks is retained. Closed and archived periods keep their reviews read-only.

### A safer end-of-cycle checkpoint

Closing a period now checks for a retrospective. If one is missing, the close dialog offers a direct path to create it. Teams can still close without a retrospective when an exception is necessary, but only after an explicit confirmation.

Rollover and templates are weight-aware. Rolled KRs keep their weights and current progress while starting the new cycle without historical check-ins or a stale blocker signal.

## Additional improvements

- Added English and Simplified Chinese copy for weights, health states, risk reasons, and review workflows.
- Added responsive styling for health badges and review dialogs.
- Updated both READMEs with exact formulas, lifecycle rules, storage examples, compatibility guidance, and FAQs.
- Upgraded the period-template schema to version 2 while retaining read compatibility with version 1 templates.

## Compatibility

- Minimum Obsidian version remains `1.7.2`.
- No migration is required for 1.3.x Objective files.
- Missing weights default to `1`.
- Missing blocker state is derived from the latest check-in.
- Version 1 period templates remain supported with default weight `1`.

Before downgrading below 1.4.0, back up or commit the vault. Older versions ignore review files and may discard the new KR weight or blocker fields when rewriting an Objective.

## Verification

The release candidate passes 83 automated tests covering weighted aggregation, health rules, old-file compatibility, check-in blocker updates, template and rollover behavior, structured review round-trips, immutable snapshots, duplicate prevention, and lifecycle permissions. It also passes ESLint, TypeScript checking, the production build, and whitespace validation.

## Installation

Install from Obsidian's community plugin directory when available, or copy `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` from the release assets into:

```text
<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/vault-okr-manager/
```

Download the three individual assets above; GitHub's automatic **Source code** archives are not installable plugin builds. Reload Obsidian after replacing the files.
